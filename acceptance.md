# Acceptance

- [ ] A played game carries both scores and an unplayed one carries neither; a half-typed score is refused. {#accept-score-whole}
- [ ] A game between a team and itself is refused. {#accept-no-self-game}
- [ ] A championship states its own points for a win, a draw and a loss, three, one and none unless it says otherwise. {#accept-points-rule}
- [ ] A championship's season reads "2026" when it begins and ends in one year and "2026/2027" when it spans two. {#accept-season}
- [ ] A player's position is one of the eight the archive knows, or none. {#accept-position}
- [ ] Extra-time and penalty scores come in pairs, and only on a played game. {#accept-extra-time}
- [ ] A season never ends before it begins; a one-day tournament is a season. {#accept-season-order}
- [ ] A zone of a table runs from a place down to a place at or below it. {#accept-zone-order}
- [ ] A goal is a penalty, an own goal or neither — never both. {#accept-goal-kind}
- [ ] A phase's table breaks ties by the archive's own keys — points, wins, goal difference, goals for, shoot-out goals, away goals, head-to-head, name — in the order the phase states, upstream's order unless it says otherwise. {#accept-tiebreak}
- [ ] A goal counts for the home or the away side of its game, and nothing else. {#accept-goal-side}
- [ ] The front page lists every tournament beginning less than thirty days ahead and ending more than thirty days ago, grouped into national, continental and world sections in that order. Each section is sorted by upstream’s geometric weighted team rating, and every tournament links to its page. {#accept-home-levels}
- [ ] The front page leads with up to twenty important upcoming fixtures and twenty recently played games across competitions in the declared fourteen-day windows, with a three-hour upcoming grace period, with competition, Brasília date/time, results and links to game details. Selection follows the reference's team strength, match importance and daily proximity decay around its three-hour-shifted clock. Global rank order is retained under one date heading with the localized day of the week per feed/day and headings at phase transitions. Highlight the union of the five displayed games with greatest raw quality and the greatest weighted-quality game per phase/date, with semibold primary-color team names and bold theme-accent scores and separators against quieter ordinary names and scores; empty windows say so. {#accept-home-games}
- [ ] Below the game feeds, the front page keeps the season an editor features, its top six with each side's title chance, and the way to its chances. {#accept-home-featured}
- [ ] The catalogue lists every championship with its level and its category, "Profissional" when it names none. {#accept-catalog}
- [ ] Typing part of a name or picking a level narrows the catalogue as the reader types, and a search that matches nothing says so. Text searches ignore case and diacritics and treat ı as i, Þ/þ as th, and æ as ae. {#accept-catalog-search}
- [ ] A championship's page shows its full name and season, its level, its category, the points for a win, a draw and a loss, and every phase in order with its groups, their teams and their zones; a phase with no groups says so. {#accept-championship-page}
- [ ] Every page reads in Portuguese, Spanish and English at its own address, and switching language keeps the reader on the same page. {#accept-languages}
- [ ] Every page renders in a dark appearance when the reader's system asks for one, with the same hierarchy and readable contrast. {#accept-dark}
- [ ] Every page is readable without sideways scrolling on a phone (390px), a tablet (768px), a laptop (1366px) and a desktop (1920px). {#accept-screen-range}
- [ ] An address that names no championship says so in words rather than showing an empty page. {#accept-championship-gone}
- [ ] A group's table is recounted from its games — points, games, wins, draws, losses, goals and position by the phase's own tie-breaks — and matches the table golaberto.com.br shows for the same games. {#accept-standings}
- [ ] A result recorded after the fact moves the table for every reader without a reload. {#accept-standings-live}
- [ ] The games page lists the upcoming games across the archive, soonest first, each with its championship, teams and Brasília kick-off. {#accept-games-upcoming}
- [ ] The games page's results tab lists the latest played games with their scores, and a reader who comes back finds the tab they left. {#accept-games-results}
- [ ] A game's page shows its championship and phase, the score with extra time and penalties when there were any, its round, day, kick-off, stadium, referee and attendance, its goals by minute on their side, and both line-ups with minutes, cards and contribution. Each side's five preceding results and five prior same-category meetings follow the original ordering. Valid team coordinates show distance, an attributed embedded OpenStreetMap and a separate directions link; missing coordinates say so. Historical prematch odds use only ratings strictly before the match. A timeline appears only when ratings and source-event minutes are usable, and hypothetical goals or red cards can each be added, removed, reassigned and retimed. Unavailable probability states never fabricate a graph. {#accept-game-page}
- [ ] An address that names no game says so in words. {#accept-game-gone}
- [ ] A championship's page shows each phase's current round and the next, with their games. {#accept-rounds}
- [ ] The teams page lists every team by latest rating descending, unrated last and ties by name/id, displaying rating, city and country. Country/region and name searches combine, ignoring case and diacritics. Both reset pagination and survive detail/back within the tab; phones retain rating and country. {#accept-teams}
- [ ] A team's main profile shows its full name, city, country, foundation, stadium and latest rating; current/upcoming and past championships link to separate team-championship pages. Current players are deduplicated across non-ended championships; past players have no current association. Rating history has period choices, an inclusive exact date range with reset and invalid-range feedback, and exact values; player history search ignores accents. All-competition matches are pageable and filterable; recorded coordinates link to a map. Team discussion preserves refused drafts and permits signed-in authors. {#accept-team-page}
- [ ] A team in one championship shows only its fixtures/results, full tables of its relevant groups, registered and observed roster with games/minutes/goals/cards, and detailed zone and final-position probabilities, including an inclusive final-position range with selected aggregate, reset and invalid-range feedback. Readers can switch teams within the championship, filter matches by phase and home/away, search and sort the roster, and compare points/position charts with links to games. Category is fixed and is not shown or selectable. Fixtures show actual home then away names and scores, without repeating the championship name. Fixture and table team names are ordinary links without arrows, remain on one line with ellipsis when needed, and expose their full name on hover. Recorded contributions and per-90 metrics have full-roster totals, and recorded zone odds have history charts. Long lists remain pageable; language switches preserve both IDs. Missing tables, rosters or probabilities say so; zero probabilities remain zero. {#accept-team-championship-page}
- [ ] A pair of team and championship with no recorded connection says so in words. {#accept-team-championship-gone}
- [ ] A player's page shows their name, position and country, a line for each championship and team they played for, and the games they played. {#accept-player-page}
- [ ] A player's season is recounted from the line-ups and goals recorded, and matches the player table golaberto.com.br shows for the same games. {#accept-player-stats}
- [ ] A goal or an appearance recorded after the fact moves the player's season for every reader without a reload. {#accept-player-stats-live}
- [ ] An address that names no team, or no player, says so in words. {#accept-team-gone}
- [ ] The stadiums page lists every stadium with its city and country, and the referees page every referee with where they are from; typing part of a name narrows either as the reader types, ignoring case and diacritics. {#accept-venues}
- [ ] A stadium's page shows its name, full name, city and country, the teams that play there, and the games played there, newest first, each leading to its page. {#accept-stadium-page}
- [ ] A referee's page shows their name, where they are from, and the games they refereed, newest first, each leading to its page. {#accept-referee-page}
- [ ] A game's page names its stadium and its referee, each leading to their page. {#accept-game-venue}
- [ ] An address that names no stadium, or no referee, says so in words. {#accept-venue-gone}
- [ ] A reader signs in with one gesture and a passkey, and keeps the handle they had as a guest. {#accept-sign-in}
- [ ] A signed-in reader comments on a game; the comment appears for every reader of the game with the author's handle, newest first, without a reload. {#accept-comment}
- [ ] A comment that cannot be posted — empty, too long, or written by a reader who has not signed in — is refused with a sentence saying why, and what was typed is kept. {#accept-comment-refused}
- [ ] An editor corrects a game from its page — the score, whether it was played, the crowd, the stadium, the referee, and its goals, each added for a player of the line-ups or removed — and the game's page and the scorers' seasons follow without a reload; a reader who is not an editor is not offered the editor. Stadium and referee searches ignore case and diacritics. {#accept-edit-game}
- [ ] A correction the archive refuses — saved by an account that is not an editor, or a goal with no scorer — is refused with a sentence saying why, and the edits are kept. {#accept-edit-refused}
- [ ] A reader opens a group's chances from its table: each team's chance of ending in each of the group's zones, and of each final position, worked out from the season so far and the same for every reader; when a result is recorded the chances follow on an open page. {#accept-chances}
- [ ] A team's page shows its rating and a player's page his, both refit from the archive as results are recorded. {#accept-ratings}
- [ ] An editor creates and updates teams, players, stadiums, referees, matches and championship structures; records goals, lineups and player registrations; sets scoring, tiebreaks, adjustments and overlapping sparse zones; and merges duplicate players without losing their archive. Related-record searches return at most forty choices while retaining the selected record; inline creation returns the new record to the form. A phase clone copies its championship rules, groups, zones and members into generated identifiers with its phase at position 1 and without games or player registrations. Zero scores and attendance remain zero. Writes preserve creator attribution and record the latest editor. {#accept-everyday-editing}
- [ ] Guests and signed-in readers without an editor grant cannot use archive or competition editing forms or write through their endpoints. Refused writes preserve the form; malformed sparse positions and attempts to change attribution fail, and deleting a record referenced by the archive is refused rather than silently erasing dependent history. {#accept-editing-grant}
- [ ] Each account has a public profile with its display name, location, biography and counts of its comments and game edits; only its signed-in owner can create or change the biography. {#accept-community-profile}
- [ ] Guests and other accounts see no controls to edit someone else's biography or delete someone else's comment, and a direct write is refused with the comment and biography unchanged; an author can delete their own comment after confirming. {#accept-community-ownership}
- [ ] A game's history lists every edit, newest first and forty to a page, with who made it, when, and each changed field's old and new value as plain text; the same edits appear on the author's profile. {#accept-community-history}
- [ ] An account owner uploads and removes their avatar, and an editor attaches a team logo; other accounts see no upload controls, and published images keep their address and are never handed to another upload. {#accept-community-media}
