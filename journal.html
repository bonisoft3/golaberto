2026-10-01 · Initial Brief

GolAberto

GolAberto is an open database of football: every championship, its phases and groups, every game with its goals and line-ups, and the tables those games make. Nothing a reader sees is typed twice: a table is what its games say; a player's goals are the goals recorded in games; a referee's record is the games he whistled.

Data

  • Category, Championship, Phase, Group, Zone, TeamGroup — competition structure, standings sorting rules, and qualification/relegation zones.
  • Team, Stadium, Referee, Player, PlayerStat, TeamPlayer — clubs, national teams, venues, referees, and squad statistics recounted from recorded line-ups and goals.
  • Game, Goal, PlayerGame, Comment — match fixtures, scores, minute-by-minute events, line-ups, and community discussion.
